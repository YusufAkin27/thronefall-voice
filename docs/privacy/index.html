Thronefall – Privacy Policy

Türkçe

Effective date: 10 October 2026 Developer: Yusuf Akın ("we") Contact: ysufakin23@gmail.com

This policy describes what information the Android game Thronefall (package com.thronefall.game, "the Game") handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to version 1.x of the Game.

1. Summary

  • Thronefall is a single-player story game. It has no user accounts, no chat and no social features, and we run no servers of our own.
  • This version contains no advertising. It offers one optional one-time purchase (Premium), sold through Google Play (see section 5).
  • The Game contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and nothing you do in the Game is sent to us.

2. Data stored only on your device

Data Purpose Leaves the device?
Save file (current reign, choices, chronicle, unlocked endings) Continue where you left off No (see Android backup below)
Settings (language, sound, voice, text speed, accessibility) Remember your preferences No (see Android backup below)
Downloaded voice-pack files Play character voices No

You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Reset progress, with Settings → Voice packs → Delete (voice files), by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the save file and settings in your device backup so that your progress returns when you reinstall or change phones. This backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account and Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. Downloaded voice files are excluded from backup.

3. Optional voice packs

Character voice-over (Turkish, English, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese; French coming) is an optional download of audio files. However you installed the Game (Google Play included), the voice pack of your language is downloaded over HTTPS from a public GitHub repository (github.com/YusufAkin27/thronefall-voice, GitHub Releases), under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Game contacts no other server for this.

As with any web download, GitHub (the service delivering the file) receives technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address and device/network information. The Game sends no personal data with these requests, and we do not receive this data. The download can start automatically on Wi-Fi; on mobile data the Game asks you first. You can delete a voice pack at any time in Settings → Voice packs.

AI-generated voices. The character voices were produced with AI text-to-speech. They are not recordings of real people. The Game does not use your microphone and never records your voice.

4. Permissions

The Game requests only these Android permissions:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: downloading voice packs and checking whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • VIBRATE: optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: buying the optional Premium through Google Play.

The Game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos/media, phone, or the advertising ID.

5. Premium purchase

Premium (no ads, voice packs, premium card and app design) is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see them. The Game only receives from Google Play a confirmation that the purchase is valid, checks it on your device, and stores on your device the fact that Premium is unlocked. Purchases are restored automatically through your Google account when you reinstall the Game. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

5a. What we do not do

We do not show ads, build profiles, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics. The Unity engine's hardware statistics and crash-reporting services are switched off.

6. Google Play

Installing and updating the Game through Google Play is governed by Google's terms and Privacy Policy. Google gives developers aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example install counts, crash and "app not responding" reports) in the Play Console. These do not identify you.

Ratings. After a long reign or a newly discovered ending, the Game may ask Google Play to show its standard rating card (at most about once a month; Google decides whether it actually appears). Settings → Rate Thronefall opens the Game's Play Store page. Ratings and reviews are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy; the Game does not see whether or how you rated it.

7. Children

Thronefall is a game for teens and adults. It contains mature themes described in text (war, executions, assassination, political intrigue). It is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the Game collects no personal data at all, no data about a child is ever sent to us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete; all Game data stays on your device and can be deleted as described in section 2. For data held by Google or GitHub, use the links above. For any question about the Game, write to us.

9. Security

Voice packs are downloaded over encrypted HTTPS connections and their integrity is checked (SHA-256) before use. Locally stored data is kept in the Game's private app storage.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that process personal data (for example optional ads), we will update this policy before that version is released, change the effective date above and mention it in the release notes.
