Thronefall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Akın · Contact: ysufakin23@gmail.com · Türkçe

This policy describes how the Android game Thronefall (package com.thronefall.game, "the Game") handles information.

1. Summary

  • Thronefall is a single-player, offline story game. It has no user accounts, no chat and no social features, and we run no servers.
  • The current version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (save file), settings (language, sound, text speed, accessibility) and the list of unlocked endings are stored locally in the Game's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them.

3. Voice packs

Optional voice-over packs are downloaded when you choose to use them: from Google Play (Play Asset Delivery) when the Game is installed from Google Play, otherwise from a public GitHub repository (github.com/YusufAkin27/thronefall-voice). As with any download, these services receive technical data such as your IP address and device/network information under their own privacy policies (Google, GitHub). We do not receive this data.

4. Google Play

Installing and updating the Game through Google Play is governed by Google's privacy policy. Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example crash reports and install counts) through the Play Console.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all locally stored data at any time from the Game's settings ("Reset progress") or by uninstalling the Game. For questions, contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

If a future version adds features that process personal data (for example optional ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
