Thronefall: Privacy Policy

Türkçe

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Developer: Yusuf Akın ("we")

Contact: ysufakin23@gmail.com

This policy describes what information the Android game Thronefall (package com.thronefall.game, "the Game") handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to version 1.x of the Game.

1. Summary

  • Thronefall is a single-player story game. It has no user accounts, no chat and no social features, and we run no servers of our own.
  • This version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • The Game contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and nothing you do in the Game is sent to us.

Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the save file and

settings in your device backup so that your progress returns when you reinstall or change phones. This backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account and Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. Downloaded voice files are excluded from backup.

3. Optional voice packs

Character voice-over (currently Turkish) is an optional download of audio files:

  • If you installed the Game from Google Play, the voice pack is delivered by Google Play (Play Asset Delivery), under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • If the Game was installed outside Google Play (for example a manually installed APK), the voice pack is downloaded over HTTPS from a public GitHub repository (github.com/YusufAkin27/thronefall-voice), under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

As with any download, the service delivering the file (Google or GitHub) receives technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address and device/network information. The Game sends no personal data with these requests, and we do not receive this data. The download can start automatically on Wi-Fi; on mobile data the Game asks you first. You can delete a voice pack at any time in Settings → Voice packs.

AI-generated voices. The character voices were produced with AI text-to-speech. They are not recordings of real

people. The Game does not use your microphone and never records your voice.

4. Permissions

The Game requests only these Android permissions:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: downloading voice packs and checking whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • VIBRATE: optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: added by Google's Play Asset Delivery and Android WorkManager libraries so that a voice-pack download you started can finish and be unpacked even if you leave the Game. They are not used for anything else.

The Game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos/media, phone, or the advertising ID.

5. What we do not do

We do not show ads, sell anything inside the Game, build profiles, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party analytics. The Unity engine's hardware statistics and crash-reporting services are switched off.

6. Google Play

Installing and updating the Game through Google Play is governed by Google's terms and Privacy Policy. Google gives developers aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example install counts, crash and "app not responding" reports) in the Play Console. These do not identify you.

7. Children

Thronefall is a game for teens and adults. It contains mature themes described in text (war, executions, assassination, political intrigue). It is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the Game collects no personal data at all, no data about a child is ever sent to us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete; all Game data stays on your device and can be deleted as described in section 2. For data held by Google or GitHub, use the links above. For any question about the Game, write to us.

9. Security

Voice packs are downloaded over encrypted HTTPS connections and their integrity is checked (SHA-256) before use. Locally stored data is kept in the Game's private app storage.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that process personal data (for example optional ads or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released, change the effective date above and mention it in the release notes.
